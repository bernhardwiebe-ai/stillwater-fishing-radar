# Data and licensing notes

- **Open-Meteo** supplies forecast data. Review its current attribution and commercial-use terms before production launch.
- **OpenStreetMap** data is © OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL. Attribution is displayed in the map and footer.
- **Nominatim** is used for development geocoding. Its public service has usage limits and is not appropriate as an unrestricted commercial backend.
- Species and lure guidance is original product copy based on general freshwater angling practice. It is heuristic—not proof of species presence, current regulation, or catch probability.
- The current app has no retailer/manufacturer photography. `icons/stillwater-icon.svg` is a product-created vector mark. Future illustrations require source, creator, license, alt text, and commerce permissions in structured metadata.
